Delhi HC calls for audit of hostels; collapse toll hits 7, Pg1

Delhi High Court orders audit of all PG hostels after building collapse kills seven, raising concerns over student safety and municipal oversight.

GS 2: Governance GS 2: Social Justice GS 3: Disaster Management GS 2: Polity

Key Highlights:

  • A building collapse in the Satya Niketan area of Delhi resulted in seven fatalities.
  • The Delhi High Court directed the Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD) to inspect all paying guest (PG) hostels under its jurisdiction within a week.
  • The incident has raised significant concerns regarding student safety, unregulated construction, and the lack of proper municipal oversight in densely populated areas.
  • Authorities have been asked to submit a detailed report outlining the structural safety of these accommodations and any violations of building bylaws.
  • The court emphasized that student lives cannot be put at risk due to administrative negligence and demanded strict action against illegal PG operations.
